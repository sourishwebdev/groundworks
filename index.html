What we take on

Services built around small teams

Most partners need a credible site before they can fundraise or recruit at scale. We focus on foundations that last—clear story, fast pages, and content you can update.

  • 01

    Discovery & IA

    Stakeholder interviews, sitemap, and messaging hierarchy so visitors understand your impact in seconds.

  • 02

    Visual identity refresh

    Logo polish, color and type direction, and reusable components that feel cohesive across print and web.

  • 03

    Marketing site build

    Multi-page or single-page sites with performance budgets, semantic HTML, and WCAG AA as our baseline.

  • 04

    Donations & email capture

    Integration guidance for your processor or platform—embedded forms, receipts, and donor journeys that stay trustworthy.

  • 05

    Content migration

    Moving legacy pages, cleaning PDFs into HTML where it helps, and redirects so bookmarks and search keep working.

  • 06

    Launch playbook

    Analytics setup, basic SEO checklist, editor training, and a short maintenance guide so your team stays confident.

How it works

Four beats from hello to handoff

We keep cycles predictable so volunteers can plan around day jobs—and so your board always knows what’s next.

  1. 1

    Intake form

    Tell us your mission, audience, timeline, and any must-have integrations. We reply within five business days.

  2. 2

    Fit call

    A 30-minute conversation to confirm scope, assets, and whether we’re the right volunteer match for this season.

  3. 3

    Design & build sprints

    Weekly async updates and two live reviews. You’ll see wireframes, then high-fidelity screens, then the working site in staging.

  4. 4

    Launch & documentation

    DNS cutover, monitoring checks, and a concise handoff packet: credentials map, editor walkthrough, and where to ask follow-up questions.
