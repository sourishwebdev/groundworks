Season at a glance

Key dates for families planning around school and travel. Final field assignments post in GotSport closer to kickoff.

Important season milestones
Milestone Window Notes
Fall recreation registration Early July – mid August Age groups U4–U19; buddy requests where space allows.
Travel tryouts (Saints FC) Late May – early June Rosters and team placement communicated by coaching staff.
Fall league first kick Weekend after Labor Day Schedules distributed via email and GotSport.
Spring recreation window January – February signup Shorter season; great for multi-sport athletes.
Winter training / futsal blocks December – March Optional add-ons; announced by team managers.

Teams & divisions

Pick the pathway that fits your player. Questions about placement? Ask at registration—we answer parent email daily during peak signup.

  • Micro & junior rec

    Ages 3–8

    Introductory games, small-sided fields, and volunteer coaches focused on touches, joy, and showing up ready to play.

    Join rec league

  • Competitive rec

    Ages 9–14

    More structured practice blocks, refereed matches, and a bridge toward travel for players who want a little more edge.

    See availability

  • Saints FC travel

    U9–U19

    Premier training rhythm, league and tournament travel, and staff who treat development and sportsmanship as non-negotiable.

    Tryouts & teams

  • High school prep

    U15+

    Faster pace, film-friendly habits, and leadership reps for athletes aiming at school ball and beyond.
